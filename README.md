**Asteroids Shooter**

For this game we replicated the famous asteroid shooting game. You have to shoot the asteroids to get points and they split into two when you hit them. Also they will respawn in waves and there will be an invisible enemy ship that will chase you and shoot you. If you get shot, touched the asteroids, or touched the enemy ship you lose.

Controls:
 - up arrow for acceleration (if you let go the acceleration decreases and the ship stops)
 - left and right arrow for turning left or right
 - down arrow for slowing down and stopping
 - space for shooting
